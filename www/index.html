Import WSJT-X decodes

Paste decode lines from WSJT-X (the Band Activity pane, or lines from ALL.txt). Einstein parses mode, SNR, dT, frequency and the callsign/grid from the message, and adds them to your log — a cross-platform way to capture real FT8/FT4/Q65/MSK144/WSPR decodes without extra software.

Live capture over WSJT-X's UDP broadcast, rigctld (Hamlib) CAT control, and PSK Reporter spotting need raw network sockets — those run in the desktop (Windows/Linux) build's helper and are the next phase; the log, analysis and import here work on every platform.

Einstein Email & Modem

Clear-text RF email / messaging over an SSB rig, carried by the built-in Einstein Modem (adaptive OFDM). Compose here; the waveform is generated and decoded by the rf/ tools (einstein_modem.py + einstein_email.py) on the SDR/soundcard host.

How it sends

Einstein Email compresses (zlib, reversible — not a cipher), splits the message into CRC-protected blocks, and runs a selective ARQ: the receiver asks only for the blocks that failed, so traffic gets through fading that would wipe a single pass. Pro lifts the airtime governor and uses 16-QAM over ~2.4 kHz for ~5× the throughput of Standard.

Full spec, measured FER/throughput and the recommended innovations: rf/MODEM.md.

About

Einstein is a weak-signal station companion: an honest logging and analysis tool that works alongside WSJT-X and Hamlib rather than re-implementing their decoders. Modes you log include WSPR, FT8, FT4, MSK144, Q65, FST4W, JT65, 2 m / 70 cm EME, meteor scatter and troposcatter. Data stays on your device; export ADIF for your main logbook.
